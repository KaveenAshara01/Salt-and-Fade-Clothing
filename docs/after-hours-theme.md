# After-hours cinematic direction

Inspired by the jewel-store colour and contrast of Uncut Gems and the glamorous villa atmosphere of POPSTAR. No film stills, music-video frames, celebrity likenesses, or third-party artwork used.

Set AFTER_HOURS_ENABLED = false in frontend/src/theme.js to restore the warm vintage styling and original daytime pool image. Set INDIE_POP_ENABLED = false as well to restore the earlier colourful luxury baseline.

The theme is scoped in frontend/src/after-hours.css. Product photography is unchanged. New generated asset: frontend/public/images/pool-after-hours.png. Mode: built-in imagegen.

Generation prompt:
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Wide landscape 3:2 campaign background for independent surf streetwear label Salt & Fade. An expensive but lived-in coastal villa swimming pool at blue hour, emerald underwater pool lights, cobalt twilight sky, warm amber interior windows, palm silhouettes, chrome ladder catching a harsh on-camera flash, tiny magenta reflected highlights, a rumpled ivory towel and translucent orange cup near edge. Candid late night 35mm flash photograph, slightly imperfect framing, fine authentic film grain, cinematic contrast, tangible water reflections, realistic architecture. Opulent, energetic, intimate, not sterile hotel brochure. Mostly dark open pool water in left half to place white website copy. No people, no garments, no words, no logos, no watermark. Avoid CGI, glossy 3D render, excessive bloom, neon rainbow gradients.
